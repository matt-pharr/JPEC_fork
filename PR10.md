## Goal
Assemble and solve the global linear system, then produce solution outputs equivalent to RDCON.

---

## Scope and deliverables

### 1) Global solve
- Implement banded or sparse linear solve for the Galerkin system.

### 2) Solution extraction
- Convert the solved DOFs into physical solution vectors and diagnostic outputs.

### 3) Output generation
- Write solution outputs in a stable format matching Fortran.

---

## Fortran subroutines to convert
- `gal_solve`
- `gal_get_solution`
- `gal_output_solution`
- `gal_write_delta`
